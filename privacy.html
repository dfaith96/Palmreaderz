Privacy policy.

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This policy explains how Palmreaderz handles information when you use our website and palm-reading demo.

Information we collect

We may collect the email address you enter to receive a reading, messages you send through our contact form, and technical information such as browser type and usage data. Palm photos are processed to provide your reading experience.

How we use information

We use your information to provide and improve the service, send the reading you requested, respond to your messages, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Photos and email delivery

Only upload photos you have permission to use. If you request email delivery, we send the reading to the email address you provide through our email delivery provider. Do not send sensitive information through the contact form.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us run the website, process requests, and deliver email. They may use the information only to provide those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we use reasonable safeguards to protect information.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us. You can also choose not to provide an email address; this only affects email delivery of your reading.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as our service changes. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@palmreaderz.it.com.
